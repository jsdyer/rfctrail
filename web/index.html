SOURCES & INTERPRETATION

The series is bigger than standards.

Read the status

RFCs include Internet Standards, Proposed Standards, Best Current Practices, Informational and Experimental documents, Historic material and older records with unknown status. A high RFC number is not a quality rating. Publication and current status can differ.

The trail shows formal update/replacement relationships, not every citation or every idea that influenced a document. A linked draft is the precursor named by the RFC Editor; complete draft revisions and process history live in the Datatracker.
